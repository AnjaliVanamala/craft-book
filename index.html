Craft #1

Sketchbook Walk

What we need

  • Sketchbook
  • Sketch pencils
  • Eraser
  • Pencil sharpener
  • A nice day
4

Sketchbook Walk

We'll pick a nice day, pick up our sketchbooks and pencils and just start walking around enjoying the nature! If there's anything we find pretty, or catches our eye, we can stop and sketch it.

The sketches don't have to be perfect. In fact, i'd argue sketches shouldn't be! The whole point is to find beauty in our surroundings, notice things we haven't noticed before, learn more about what we each find beauty in, and go on a fun walk together!

When we're done:
We can share our sketches and pick out our favorites. We could frame them, hang them up, or trade with each other!

Craft #2

Canvas Switch Painting Night

What we need

  • 2 canvases
  • Acrylic paint
  • Paint brushes
  • Acrylic markers
6

Canvas Switch

We each start with a canvas and set a timer for 5 minutes. We start painting whatever comes to mind, blobs, hearts, a landspace, whatever!

When the timer goes off, we switch canvases and try to expand on what the other person started.

We keep switching every 5 minutes until both paintings are finished. (Finished is just up to us, we can stop whenever lol).

After:
We can pick our favorites to keep, then we both have a reminder of a super fun night!
