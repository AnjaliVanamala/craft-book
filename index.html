Sketchbook Walk

We'll walk around and make quick sketches of things we find pretty, interesting, or just worth stopping to look at.

They don't have to be perfect. The whole point is just to notice things and draw whatever catches our attention.

When we're done:
We can share our sketches and pick out our favorites to frame or trade with each other.

+

Add inspiration photo here

What we need

  • 2 canvases
  • Acrylic paint
  • Paint brushes
  • Acrylic markers
  • Me
5

Canvas Switch

We each start with a canvas and set a timer for 5 minutes.

When the timer goes off, we switch canvases and keep painting.

We keep switching every 5 minutes until both paintings are finished.

The rule:
You have to trust the other person to add whatever they want to your painting.
